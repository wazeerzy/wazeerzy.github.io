About Wazeerzy

Supporting Medicine Supply Through Wholesale Service

Wazeerzy Medicine Wholesale is focused on supplying medicines and healthcare products to pharmacies, wholesalers, and healthcare businesses.

Who We Are

Wazeerzy Medicine Wholesale is a medicine wholesale business based in Afghanistan. We focus on wholesale supply, product inquiries, bulk orders, and customer service for pharmacy and healthcare businesses.

Our goal is to make medicine procurement more convenient by providing clear product information, wholesale communication, and easy ordering through WhatsApp.

Our Mission

To build a reliable and accessible wholesale supply network that helps pharmacies and healthcare businesses obtain the products they need.

Our Vision

To develop a professional medicine wholesale and distribution network serving customers throughout Afghanistan.

  • Wholesale-focused customer service
  • Easy WhatsApp ordering and inquiries
  • Product and price-list support
  • Long-term business relationships
